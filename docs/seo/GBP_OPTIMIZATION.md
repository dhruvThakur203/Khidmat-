## 2. Primary category recommendation

### Business reality

Khidmat operates as:
1. **Restaurant** — dine-in at Kalkaji (Delhi) and Sector 50 (Noida)
2. **Catering services** — weddings, corporate, parties, private gatherings across Noida & Delhi NCR

### Recommendation by listing

| Location | Recommended primary | Recommended secondary | Reasoning |
|----------|--------------------|-----------------------|-----------|
| **Noida (Sector 50)** | **Caterer** | Restaurant, Indian restaurant, Wedding buffet (if available) | Primary growth objective is catering searches (“caterers in Noida”, “catering services near me”). Noida is the stated primary market. Caterer category aligns with Maps intent for event catering. |
| **Delhi (Kalkaji)** | **Restaurant** | Caterer, Indian restaurant, Mughlai restaurant (if available) | This location is primarily a dine-in restaurant. Restaurant as primary avoids misleading Maps users searching for a nearby place to eat. |

### Why not “Restaurant” as primary everywhere?

If catering lead generation is the main objective, a Restaurant-primary listing ranks better for “restaurant near me” but weaker for “caterer near me”. Splitting category strategy by location reflects how customers actually find each branch.

### What NOT to do

- Do not use “Event planner” or “Wedding planner” unless Khidmat genuinely offers full event planning beyond food
- Do not add categories for services not provided
- Do not create fake virtual offices for service areas

📋 **Business owner:** Confirm categories in GBP Manager → Edit profile → Business category. Google’s available categories vary — choose the closest accurate match.

## 5. Photo acquisition strategy

Real photos since 1992 are a competitive advantage. **No stock images.**

| Category | What to collect | Qty | Why it matters |
|----------|-----------------|-----|----------------|
| **1. Exterior / entrance** | Restaurant façade, signage, entrance at both branches | 3–5 per location | Confirms legitimacy; improves “near me” trust |
| **2. Restaurant interior** | Dining room, seating, ambience, heritage details | 8–12 | Supports restaurant category and dine-in intent |
| **3. Food & signature dishes** | Dal Khidmat, butter chicken, biryani, kebabs, desserts | 15–20 | High engagement; appears in food searches |
| **4. Wedding catering** | Buffet lines, live counters, wedding setups (with permission) | 10–15 | Directly supports “wedding catering Noida” |
| **5. Corporate events** | Office buffets, conference catering, professional setup | 8–10 | Supports corporate catering intent |
| **6. Birthday / party** | Party tables, celebration spreads | 6–8 | Party catering relevance |
| **7. Private gatherings** | Home events, intimate setups | 5–8 | Private catering relevance |
| **8. Buffet displays** | Chafing dishes, Indian buffet presentation | 8–10 | Universal catering proof |
| **9. Kitchen / behind the scenes** | Kitchen prep, tandoor, chefs at work (no faces without consent) | 6–8 | E-E-A-T — real operations |
| **10. Team at work** | Staff serving, setting up events (with permission) | 4–6 | Humanises the brand |
| **11. Customer events** | Real events with written permission only | Ongoing | Strongest social proof |

### Photo upload cadence

- **Month 1:** Upload 30+ existing photos across categories
- **Ongoing:** 4–8 new photos per month from real events
- **GBP posts:** Reuse best photos with short captions linking to website pages

📋 **Business owner:** Assign one team member to collect photos after each catered event.
