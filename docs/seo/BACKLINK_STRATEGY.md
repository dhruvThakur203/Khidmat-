## Part B — Backlink acquisition strategy

Backlinks = other websites linking to khidmat.co.in. They build domain authority.

### Target pages for links

| Page | Best for links from… |
|------|---------------------|
| `/noida-catering` | General catering directories, local business features |
| `/wedding-catering-noida` | Wedding venues, planners, bridal blogs |
| `/corporate-catering-noida` | Corporate event partners, business associations |
| `/` | General brand mentions, press features |
| `/about-khidmat` | Heritage / “since 1992” stories |

### Strategy 1: Wedding venue & planner partnerships

**Value Khidmat offers:** Reliable catering partner with 34+ years experience; reduces venue’s vendor risk.

**Approach:**
1. Identify 10–15 Noida/GNoida venues Khidmat has already catered at
2. Ask venue manager for a “Preferred caterers” or “Vendor partners” page link
3. Offer reciprocal: feature venue on Khidmat events page (with permission)

**Link target:** `/wedding-catering-noida`

📋 Business owner action | 🌐 Requires venue website update

### Strategy 2: Corporate event partnerships

**Value:** Consistent office lunch / conference catering from an established brand.

**Approach:**
1. List 10 companies with recurring catering contracts
2. Ask HR/admin for inclusion in internal “approved vendors” wiki or employee portal
3. Offer to cater one corporate open house in exchange for a case study (not for a link directly)

**Link target:** `/corporate-catering-noida`

### Strategy 3: Local publications & food media

**Value:** Authentic Delhi/Noida food heritage story since 1992.

**Approach:**
1. Pitch “34 years of Delhi flavours” story to local food bloggers and NCR lifestyle publications
2. Offer kitchen visit or chef interview (not paid placement)
3. Ensure article links to khidmat.co.in

**Link target:** `/` or `/about-khidmat`

### Strategy 4: Event photography & vendor cross-links

**Value:** Wedding photographers and decorators need caterer referrals.

**Approach:**
1. Partner with 5–10 Noida wedding photographers
2. Mutual vendor page links on websites / Instagram link-in-bio

**Link target:** `/wedding-catering-noida`

### Strategy 5: Hospitality & business associations

**Value:** Credibility through industry membership.

**Approach:**
1. Join relevant Noida/UP hospitality or restaurant associations (if applicable)
2. Member directory links

**Link target:** `/`

### Strategy 6: Supplier & ingredient partners

**Value:** B2B relationships — dairy, equipment, packaging suppliers.

**Approach:** Low priority. Only pursue if supplier has a “clients” page.

**Link target:** `/`

### What NOT to do

- ❌ Buy backlinks
- ❌ Private blog networks (PBNs)
- ❌ Spam blog comments
- ❌ Automated link-building tools
- ❌ Link exchanges with irrelevant sites
- ❌ Press release blasts to low-quality wire services

### Backlink tracking

📋 Track monthly in a simple spreadsheet:

| Date | Source website | URL linked | Target page | Type (editorial/directory/partner) |

Goal: **2–4 quality editorial/partner links per quarter** — not hundreds of spam links.
