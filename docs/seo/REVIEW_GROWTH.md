## 1. Why reviews matter for local SEO

Google considers review **volume**, **recency**, **ratings**, and **owner responses** for local pack rankings. Khidmat’s website already links to Google and Zomato (✓ implemented). Growing genuine reviews on Google is the highest-impact off-site activity for “caterers near me” visibility.

## 2. Legitimate review acquisition process

### When to ask

| Timing | Channel | Best for |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **24–48 hours after** a successful catered event | WhatsApp | Wedding, corporate, party clients |
| **Same day** after positive dine-in feedback | In-person / receipt | Restaurant guests |
| **Follow-up** if no response after 5 days | One polite reminder only | Catering clients |

### Who should ask

- Catering coordinator or front-of-house manager — not a generic mass blast
- Only customers who had a **genuinely positive** experience
- Never ask entire guest lists — ask the **event organiser / host**

### What NOT to do

- ❌ Offer money, discounts, or free food for reviews
- ❌ Ask staff, friends, or family to post reviews
- ❌ Review-gate (only ask happy customers, suppress unhappy ones publicly)
- ❌ Post fake reviews or use review farms
- ❌ Ask for “5 stars only”

**Rules:**
- Only after genuine positive feedback
- Never pressure
- Offer WhatsApp link — don't insist

## 6. Follow-up timing strategy

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Day 0** | Event completes — internal note: client name, event type, contact |
| **Day 1–2** | Send WhatsApp thank-you + review request to organiser |
| **Day 5–7** | If no review and relationship is warm, one polite follow-up |
| **Day 8+** | No further review requests — maintain relationship for repeat business |

For **corporate clients:** ask the HR / admin contact, not individual employees.

## 7. Review response templates

Personalise every response. Replace bracketed text. Do not copy-paste identical responses in bulk.

### Positive review (4–5 stars)

```
Dear [Name], thank you for your kind words and for choosing Khidmat for your
[occasion]. We're glad [specific detail they mentioned] met your expectations.
We look forward to serving you again.
— Team Khidmat
```

### Neutral review (3 stars)

```
Dear [Name], thank you for your honest feedback. We're glad you appreciated
[positive point]. We're sorry [concern] didn't fully meet expectations — we'd
welcome the chance to discuss how we can do better. Please reach us at
info@khidmat.co.in or 99714 77902.
— Team Khidmat
```

### Negative review (1–2 stars)

```
Dear [Name], we're sorry your experience with Khidmat fell short of what we
aim to deliver. We take this seriously and would like to understand what
happened. Please contact us directly at info@khidmat.co.in or 99714 77902 so
we can address your concerns personally.
— [Manager name], Khidmat
```

### Response rules

- Respond within **48 hours**
- Never argue publicly
- Never disclose private customer details
- Invite offline resolution for serious complaints
- Do not keyword-stuff (“best caterer in Noida”) in responses
- Mention “Khidmat” naturally once at most

📋 **Business owner:** Assign one person to monitor Google and Zomato reviews weekly.
